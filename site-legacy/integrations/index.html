ReadyIQ for platforms

One readiness network. Every mortgage system.

ReadyIQ lives on its own, appears on the lender’s website, and speaks inside the CRM. One API, one small status object, adapters per platform. Consumer data stays with IDIQ; platforms receive statuses, dates, and permitted summaries.

The status object · v1
Small, stable, versioned. Every connector and every lender automation builds on it.

Three levels of integration

    

      
1 · Basic
Link + webhooks
ReadyIQ link inside the CRM, LO and lead passed in the URL, statuses back through Zapier or webhooks into custom fields. Days, not months.

      
2 · Native
Inside the workflow
SSO, a ReadyIQ card in the borrower record, automated invitations, real-time milestones, tasks and alerts, branch and LO reporting.

      
3 · Certified
Marketplace partner
Listed in the platform directory, standard install, joint enterprise customers, approved security and support model.
