ReadyIQ consumer credit tools are not a mortgage preapproval, approval, or lending decision. ReadyIQ shows a FICO® Score — not the mortgage-industry version lenders pull — and never promises deletions, points, approvals, or timelines. Consumers can apply for a mortgage at any time; nothing in ReadyIQ is required. Loan officers see status, never the report, unless the consumer requests a review.
